Adapt EQA-320 Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · Developer: Huge2na

Adapt EQA-320 is an equalizer and spectrum analyzer for Android. This policy explains what the app does, and does not do, with your information.

The short version

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. Everything it does happens on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The "microphone" permission

Android lists the permission the app needs to read the phone's own sound as "microphone" (Record audio). Adapt EQA-320 uses it only to measure the loudness of the music your phone is playing, band by band, so the analyzer bars can move with it.

  • No sound is ever recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • The measurements are used instantly to draw the display, then discarded.

Your music files

Songs and folders you choose are read only on your phone, to play them and to drive the display. Your files and their names never leave your device.

Settings stored on your phone

Your equalizer settings, memories, colors, song queue, playlists and language choice are saved on your phone only, so they are there next time you open the app. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Subscription and payments

The monthly subscription is sold and managed by Google Play. Payment details go to Google, never to the developer. The app only asks Google Play whether your subscription is active. Google's handling of your information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can cancel anytime in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Other permissions

  • Notifications: shows that the equalizer is running, as Android requires.
  • Audio settings and background playback: apply the equalizer and keep your music playing when the screen is off.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
