Shooting Bricks Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Shooting Bricks is a game made by Huge2na. This page explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

Nothing. Shooting Bricks does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress, best scores, Nuggets, ball trails and Daily Challenge streak are saved only on your own phone. They never leave it. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Permissions

The game asks to use vibration so your phone can buzz during big moments. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Questions? Email huge2na@gmail.com.
